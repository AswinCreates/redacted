# 🕵️ Redacted

> **A real-time multiplayer social deduction word game.**

**Redacted** is a multiplayer word-based social deduction game where players join the same room, receive hidden information, give clues, and try to identify the imposter.

Most players receive the same secret word, while the imposter receives only a related hint. Players must carefully give clues, observe what others say, and figure out who doesn't actually know the secret.

Think you can blend in?

**Play. Give clues. Suspect everyone. Find the imposter.**

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### 1. Create or Join a Game

One player creates a room and shares the unique room code with everyone else.

Other players can join the room using the code from their own devices.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Everyone should join the same room before the host starts the game.

---

### 2. Discover Your Secret

Once the game starts, each player privately receives their role.

**Innocent players** receive the secret word.

**Imposters** receive a related hint instead of the actual secret word.

When there are multiple imposters, they can identify each other.

> [!CAUTION]
> Never reveal your secret word directly. The imposter is listening.

---

### 3. Give Your Clue

Players take turns giving **one clue** related to the secret word.

Your clue should be specific enough to show that you know the word, but not so obvious that you give the imposter the answer.

For example, if the secret word is **"Beach"**, clues could be:

* "Sand"
* "Waves"
* "Vacation"

But giving away the word directly defeats the purpose.

---

### 4. Watch the Other Players

Pay attention to everyone's clues.

Ask yourself:

* Does their clue actually make sense?
* Are they being too vague?
* Are they copying someone else's idea?
* Does their clue sound like they only have a hint?

Sometimes the most suspicious clue is the one that sounds perfectly normal.

---

### 5. Vote

After everyone has given their clue, it's time to vote.

Each active player privately chooses the person they suspect is an imposter.

> [!WARNING]
> Choose carefully. A wrong vote can eliminate an innocent player.

---

### 6. The Reveal

The votes are revealed together.

If there is a unique player with the highest number of votes, that player is eliminated and their role is revealed.

If there is a tie for the highest number of votes, nobody is eliminated.

Eliminated players become spectators and can continue watching the game.

---

## 🎯 The Goal

### 🟢 Innocents

Your goal is to:

* protect the secret word
* give useful clues
* identify the imposter
* avoid being eliminated

### 🔴 Imposters

Your goal is to:

* figure out the secret from everyone's clues
* blend in with the other players
* avoid suspicion
* survive the vote

> [!TIP]
> As an imposter, don't try to sound like you know everything. Sometimes a carefully vague clue is safer than an overly confident one.

---

## 👥 Multiplayer

Redacted is designed to be played with friends or other players using separate devices.

Create a room, share the code, and play together in real time.

The game supports **3–20 players**, with the number of imposters depending on the number of players in the room.

---

## 💡 The Strategy

Redacted isn't just about finding the imposter.

It's about **how well you can communicate without saying too much**.

As an innocent, your clue needs to prove that you know the secret.

As an imposter, you need to understand the clues quickly enough to survive.

Every word matters.

> [!NOTE]
> The fun comes from the uncertainty. You might know the word — but can you prove that everyone else doesn't?
